Notion: Description and Rationale for Use

Notion is an all‑in‑one workspace that combines documents, tasks, databases, and wikis in a single place. It supports flexible page structures, powerful databases with views like tables, boards, calendars, and timelines, and collaborative features such as comments and mentions. This makes it well‑suited for planning, documenting, and tracking a project end‑to‑end.

Why Notion Works Best for This Project

  • Comprehensive toolset in one place: notes, tasks, wikis, and project plans live together without switching apps.
  • Agile‑friendly planning: Kanban boards, sprints, backlogs, and status workflows are easy to model with database views.
  • Dynamic project plans: plans can be updated continuously and viewed as a table, board, calendar, or timeline depending on the need.
  • Clear documentation: decisions, reasoning, and guides can be captured as linked pages and referenced from tasks.
  • Calendar and scheduling: deadlines and milestones are managed on a calendar view for quick visibility.
  • Collaboration: comments, mentions, and shared pages help align with teachers and keep feedback in context.

Documentation of the Selected Planning Tool

  • Selected tool: Notion (this page documents the choice)
  • Reasoning: Combines planning, documentation, and tracking; supports agile workflows; allows ongoing updates and clear visibility into deadlines and progress.
